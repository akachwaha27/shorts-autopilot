# Explainer topics in the Zack D Films style

Zack D Films Shorts work because each one answers a single, slightly uncomfortable "what actually happens if / how did / why does" question in under a minute, with one visual payoff. Rules of thumb for picking topics:

- The title is a question a 12-year-old would ask and an adult can't answer.
- One mechanism, one number, one "oh" moment. If it needs two explanations, it's two videos.
- Body, history, nature, machines and money all work; the hook must be visual.
- Script: hook (question, <3 s) → setup → the mechanism in 2-3 steps → the key number → payoff / takeaway. 45-60 s, 110-140 words.

## Ready-to-script topics

### History & ancient tech
1. How did ancient people keep ice all summer? (done: yakhchal)
2. How did Romans heat their floors without electricity? (hypocaust)
3. How did sailors know where they were with no GPS? (dead reckoning, sextant)
4. How did the Egyptians move 2-ton blocks? (wet sand, sledges)
5. How did people tell time at night before clocks? (water clocks, candle clocks)
6. How did medieval castles get running water?
7. Why did Roman concrete get stronger with age? (lime clasts)
8. How did Inca messengers run 240 km a day? (chasqui relay)
9. How did ancient Greeks compute eclipses? (Antikythera mechanism)
10. How did Persian wind catchers cool houses?

### Body
11. What happens to your body when you hold your breath for 2 minutes?
12. Why can't you tickle yourself?
13. What happens if you swallow gum? (nothing much, 1-2 days)
14. Why do your fingers wrinkle in water? (nerve response, grip)
15. What happens in the 10 seconds after you stand up too fast?
16. Why does your stomach growl when you're not hungry?
17. What happens to a tooth you lose as an adult?

### Nature & animals
18. How does a cat always land on its feet? (righting reflex)
19. How do birds sleep while flying? (unihemispheric sleep)
20. Why don't woodpeckers get concussions?
21. How does a tardigrade survive space?
22. Why do camels have three eyelids?

### Machines & everyday
23. What happens inside a microwave in the first second?
24. How does a plane toilet work? (vacuum)
25. Why do trains have a tiny gap between rails?
26. How does a thermos keep coffee hot for 12 hours?
27. What happens to your phone's battery at 0%?
28. How does a refrigerator make cold out of electricity?
29. How do hotel key cards know it's you?

### Money & scale
30. What happens to a $100 bill when it wears out?
31. How long would it take to count to a billion?
32. What happens if everyone withdrew their money on the same day?

- Machines → Flat-Vector Science, Synthwave Outrun, 16-Bit Pixel Odyssey
