Crows count out loud, and they plan every caw before the first one.
In Germany, scientists trained three carrion crows
to look at a number on a screen
and caw exactly that many times.
Then peck an enter key when they're done.
At first, the crows cheated.
They just kept cawing until a snack showed up.
So the team made them stop and confirm.
Now the wild part.
From the very first caw,
a computer could predict how many were coming.
The whole count was ready in their heads.
Bigger numbers made them pause longer, just like us.
So how high do you think a crow could count?
