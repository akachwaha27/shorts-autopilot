Chameleons don't change color to hide.
The real reason is way weirder,
and you've probably never heard it:
crystals that move thirty percent apart.
Under their skin are special cells,
packed with tiny crystals,
lined up in a neat grid.
When a chameleon is calm,
the crystals sit close together
and bounce back blue light.
Add the yellow skin on top,
and you get green.
But when a rival shows up,
the chameleon stretches its skin.
The crystals spread farther apart,
so now they bounce back yellow and orange.
Green turns to fiery orange
in just a couple of minutes.
It's not hiding, it's showing off.
And a deeper layer of crystals
reflects the sun's heat,
like built-in sunscreen.
So if your skin showed your mood,
what color would you be right now?
