Your window kills birds,
and they never see it coming. But why?
To a bird, your window is just more sky.
Clear glass lets a bird look straight through,
right at the plants inside.
Or the glass acts like a mirror,
showing trees and clouds behind it.
Either way, it sees an open path,
and flies full speed into a wall it never saw.
Nothing in nature is solid and see-through,
so birds never learned to expect it.
In the US,
scientists think windows kill up to a billion birds a year.
So what actually helps?
One hawk sticker barely helps.
The bird just flies around it.
But dots spaced about two inches apart
look like a fence,
so the bird turns away.
Next time you hear a thump,
go look at your window from outside.
What does a bird see in it?
