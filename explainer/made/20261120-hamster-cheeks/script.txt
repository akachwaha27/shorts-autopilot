Your hamster hides two secret pockets that nearly triple in size.
Why?
Inside each cheek is a long stretchy pouch,
a dead-end sleeve that runs all the way back to the shoulders.
Stuffed full, it balloons out like a backpack.
Some hamsters fit a whole baby carrot in there.
Wild hamsters live where food is scarce and the weather's harsh.
So they don't eat on the spot.
They fill up fast, run home,
and stash it all in an underground pantry.
To unload, they push from the back with their front paws,
and out it all pours.
A special muscle pulls the empty pouch back in.
And here's a bonus: some puff their pouches with air
to float when they swim.
So if you had two secret pockets,
what would you carry home first?
