How did people in old Japan
smell what time it was?
No gears. No ticking.
Just a box of ash.
First, they smoothed white ash flat,
and pressed a stamp into it.
That left a groove,
a maze that snakes back and forth.
They filled the groove with incense powder:
one long, thin trail.
Light one end,
and a tiny ember crawls through the maze,
at a nearly steady speed.
Here's the clever part.
At set marks along the trail,
they switched to a different incense.
So when the smell in the room changed,
you knew more time had passed.
Some trails burned for twelve hours.
Some lasted a whole month.
A few even dropped a little ball
onto a tray, like an alarm.
So, what time does your room smell like right now?
