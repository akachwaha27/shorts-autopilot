Lose your eyebrows and 54% of people can't name your face.
MIT scientists tested it in 2003.
They showed people famous faces with the eyes erased,
and they still named sixty percent.
But erase just the eyebrows,
and it dropped to forty-six.
So why do we even have them?
Picture sweat rolling down your forehead.
Your brow bone and that arch catch it like a gutter,
and the hairs all point out toward your ears,
so the drops slide off to the sides,
and not into your eyes.
Rain too.
Each brow has about 250 hairs on the job.
But their biggest job?
Talking without words.
One brow up means, really?
Both up means surprise!
Pulled down and together? Somebody's grumpy.
So can you raise just one eyebrow?
Left, right, or neither?
