Its toes are covered in tiny hairs,
about six and a half million on one gecko.

Those tips get so close to the wall
that the molecules themselves pull together.
It's called the van der Waals force.
One hair is weak,
but millions together are mighty.
To let go, a gecko just peels its toes up,
like tape.
Now scale that up to you.

about eighty percent of your front!
