Your cat can do a flip that baffled scientists in 1894.
Falling upside down, with nothing to push against,
it still turns over.
So how?
First, its inner ear senses which way is down.
Then it bends in the middle,
like two cats glued together.
It tucks its front legs in,
so the front half spins fast,
while the stretched back legs barely turn.
Then it swaps,
and the back half catches up.
A bendy spine and a loose collarbone make it possible,
all in under a second.
Kittens master it by about seven weeks.
So, have you ever seen your cat pull off the flip?
