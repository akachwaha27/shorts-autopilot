You can't regrow a finger,
but one starfish arm can grow
four new arms and a whole body.
When a crab grabs one, it can drop the arm on purpose.
A muscle at the base squeezes, and the arm lets go.
Then the wound seals itself shut,
and skin creeps over it in about two days.
Muscle cells turn back into simpler cells
and crawl toward the stump.
The first part to grow back?
A tiny tube foot at the tip.
It works like a signal tower,
telling everything behind it where to grow.
Tube feet, little bones and muscle fill in, one by one.
The whole arm takes a few months to over a year.
And in Linckia sea stars,
one arm alone can grow a brand new body.
So if one arm can make a whole starfish,
which one is the real starfish?
