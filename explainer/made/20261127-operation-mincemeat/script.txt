Britain's best spy of 1943 never said a word.
His papers said Major William Martin,
who never existed.
His pockets held love letters, theatre tickets,
and an angry tailor's bill.
A briefcase chained to his wrist held secret letters:
the Allies would hit Greece and Sardinia.
The body was really Glyndwr Michael, a homeless Welshman.
A submarine left him floating off Spain.
Spanish officers let German agents copy everything.
Hitler bought it and sent tanks to Greece.
The real target was Sicily.
The message to Churchill: Mincemeat swallowed rod, line and sinker.
So would a tailor's bill have fooled you?
