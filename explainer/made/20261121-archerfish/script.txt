This fish can.
It's called the archerfish.
It hides just under the surface,
and spots a bug sitting on a leaf above.
It doesn't jump. It spits.
So how does that work?
It presses its tongue against a groove in the roof of its mouth.
That makes a tiny tube, like a gun barrel.
Then it snaps its gill covers shut,
and water blasts out of that tube.
And here's the clever part.
The back of the jet moves faster than the front,
so the water bunches into one big blob,
right before it hits.
Big archerfish can hit a bug two to three meters away.
Smack! The bug drops,
and dinner falls right into the water.
