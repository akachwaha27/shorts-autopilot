Koalas eat poison leaves you'd never survive.
So why only eucalyptus?
Eucalyptus leaves are tough,
low in nutrients,
and toxic to most animals.
So koalas get the trees to themselves.
Their secret is inside.
A koala's gut has a pouch called the caecum,
about two metres long,
packed with millions of bacteria.
They break down the tough fibre,
and help deal with the toxins.
Even then, a koala absorbs only a quarter of it.
So it eats up to five hundred grams of leaves a day,
gets almost all its water from them,
and saves energy by sleeping,
up to twenty-two hours a day.
And baby koalas?
They eat a special mum poop called pap,
to get those gut bacteria.
So, would you eat poison leaves,
if it meant twenty-two hours of sleep?
