Zero teeth, but it'll out-eat you,
thirty thousand ants a day.
Meet the giant anteater.
First, big claws rip open the nest.
Then out comes the tongue,
two feet long,
flicking up to a hundred and fifty times a minute.
It's covered in tiny backward spines,
and thick, sticky spit,
so ants come along for the ride.
With no teeth to chew them,
it crushes them against the roof of its mouth,
and a very muscular stomach
pulverizes the rest.
Scientists think swallowed grit helps too,
a bit like a bird's gizzard.
The clever part?
It stays about a minute at each mound,
then moves on,
before the ants can fight back.
So if you had to eat thirty thousand of something,
with zero teeth, what would you pick?
