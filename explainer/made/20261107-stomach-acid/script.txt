Your stomach acid dissolves metal,
so how does your stomach survive it,
three times a day?
The acid can drop to pH one point five.
Strong enough to eat through zinc,
and turn a steak to mush.
But your stomach has three tricks.
Trick one: a thick blanket of mucus,
loaded with bicarbonate,
basically baking soda.
So right against the wall,
the acid gets neutralized.
Trick two: pepsin, the meat-eating enzyme,
is made switched off.
It only turns on out in the acid,
away from your cells.
Trick three: your lining sheds around half a million cells,
every minute,
and rebuilds itself every few days.
So damage gets replaced before it spreads.
Your stomach isn't acid-proof.
It just heals faster than it melts.
Which trick would you want: the slime, the switch, or the shedding?
