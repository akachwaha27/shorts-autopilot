Every baby tooth you ever lost,
was secretly eaten.
By what?
Kids have twenty baby teeth.
Adults have thirty-two.
But a tiny jaw can't fit big adult teeth,
so the new ones wait,
hidden in the bone underneath.
Around age six,
an adult tooth starts pushing up.
That pressure wakes up special cells,
called odontoclasts.
They dissolve the baby tooth's root,
bit by bit.
No root means nothing holding on,
so it wobbles,
and pops out.
That's why a lost baby tooth,
looks like it has no root at all.
It got eaten.
Then the adult tooth slides into the gap.
By about age twelve,
most baby teeth are gone.
So which of yours got eaten first?
