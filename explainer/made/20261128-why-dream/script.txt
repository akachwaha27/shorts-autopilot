Your brain makes two hours of movies every night.
So why does it even bother?
Most vivid dreams happen in REM sleep,
when your eyes dart around under your eyelids.
Your brain's emotion center fires up,
while the part that checks logic goes quiet.
That's why riding a giant cat to school feels totally normal.
Now the weird part.
In 2000, Harvard scientists had people play Tetris for three days.
At night, most saw falling blocks as they drifted off.
Even some patients with amnesia,
who couldn't remember playing at all.
Scientists think sleep may replay your day to save it.
So what did your brain replay last night?
