Your apple is twenty-five percent air.
Your potato isn't.
That's why one floats and one sinks.
Drop both into a bowl of water.
The apple bobs right back up.
The potato heads straight for the bottom.
The secret is density.
Anything lighter than the same amount of water floats.
Inside an apple, the cells aren't packed tightly.
Between them are tiny pockets of air,
about a quarter of the whole apple.
So the apple ends up lighter than water,
like a life jacket made of fruit.
A potato is different.
Its cells are stuffed with grains of starch,
and starch is heavier than water.
There's hardly any air in there,
so down it goes.
That's why bobbing for apples works.
If apples sank, the game would be impossible!
So what else in your kitchen
is secretly full of air?
