A pufferfish stomach stretches 100 times bigger,
and you'd never guess why it has to.
When a hungry fish gets close,
the pufferfish opens its mouth wide
and gulps seawater, fast.
Gulp after gulp,
it pumps the water into its stomach.
That stomach is folded like an accordion,
so it unfolds and keeps on stretching.
There are almost no ribs in the way,
so the whole body balloons out.
Its stretchy skin pulls tight,
and little spines pop up all over.
Now it's about three times its size,
a spiky ball
way too big to swallow.
But puffing up is hard work.
It can take hours to catch its breath,
so it only puffs when it has to.
Then it spits the water back out.
So if you were a pufferfish,
what would make you puff?
