You grew up. This axolotl refuses to, for 15 years.
Most salamanders hatch in water,
then lose their gills and crawl onto land.
But the axolotl keeps its feathery gills,
its tail fin, and its baby face,
its whole life.
The reason is a tiny switch.
Growing up needs thyroid hormone,
and an axolotl's body barely sends that signal.
So it just skips adulthood,
and still has babies of its own.
In 1865, scientists in Paris got a shock.
A few of their axolotls suddenly lost their gills,
and walked out as land salamanders.
So the switch was never broken.
Would you rather stay a kid forever, or grow up?
