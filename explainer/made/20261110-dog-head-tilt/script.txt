Why does your dog tilt its head?
Genius dogs do it twenty times more.
In Budapest, scientists tested some genius border collies
that know the names of over ten toys.
When their owner said a toy's name,
those genius dogs tilted their heads
forty-three percent of the time.
Regular dogs?
Just two percent.
So the tilt might be a dog matching your word
to a picture in its head.
But there's another idea.
A dog's snout blocks part of your face,
especially your mouth.
Tilting gives it a clearer view.
In one survey,
seventy-one percent of long-snouted dogs often tilted,
but only fifty-two percent of flat-faced dogs did.
And here's the fun part:
each dog usually tilts to the same side, every time.
So which way does your dog tilt,
left or right?
