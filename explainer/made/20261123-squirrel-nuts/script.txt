Your backyard squirrel buries 3,000 nuts a year.
How does it find them?
Each one goes in its own little hole.
Mostly, it remembers.
It builds a map in its head,
a tree, a rock, a fence post,
and that map can last up to two months.
Then its nose finds the exact spot.
But what if another squirrel is watching?
It fakes it.
It digs a hole, pretends to drop the nut,
and sneaks off with it still in its mouth.
Sneaky, right?
Still, some nuts never get dug up.
The squirrel moves on,
or it just has plenty to eat.
And that forgotten nut is already planted.
Come spring, it sprouts.
So some trees around you
were planted by a squirrel that forgot.
Which tree near you did a squirrel plant?
