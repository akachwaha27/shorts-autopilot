Hitler never won a majority.
So how did he get handed Germany?
In 1929, the Great Depression hit.
By 1932, about six million Germans had no job.
Angry voters drifted to the extremes,
and his party peaked at 37 percent.
By November, it slipped to 33.
Then a few insiders made a deal.
They got President Hindenburg to name him chancellor,
sure they could control him.
One bragged he'd be squeezed into a corner within two months.
Four weeks later, the Reichstag burned.
A decree suspended basic rights.
In March, the Enabling Act let him make laws without parliament.
So who really opened the door?
