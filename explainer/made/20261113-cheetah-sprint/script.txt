A cheetah quits after twenty seconds,
and you'd never guess why.
It hits about a hundred kilometers an hour,
zero to seventy-two in two and a half seconds.
Each stride covers seven meters,
four strides every second.
Its spine bends like a spring,
its lungs and heart are huge,
and its tail steers like a rudder.
People thought it stopped because it overheated.
But in 2013, scientists put sensors on six cheetahs.
During the chase, body heat barely changed.
It only jumped after a catch,
probably from stress, with lions nearby.
So what stops them?
That springy sprint burns energy crazy fast,
so a full sprint lasts about twenty seconds,
just a few hundred meters.
Miss by then, and the cheetah's done.
Only about half its chases end in a meal.
Would you rather be that fast for twenty seconds,
or slower all day?
