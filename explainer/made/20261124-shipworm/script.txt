But there's a problem.

So the shipworm keeps helpers in its gills.
Bacteria that make wood-melting enzymes.
They send those enzymes down to the gut,
and the clam turns timber into food.
Its tail stays at the hole, breathing seawater.
That's how shipworms chewed through wooden ships for centuries.
