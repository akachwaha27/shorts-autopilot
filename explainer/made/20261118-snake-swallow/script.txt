First, a myth.
Snakes don't unhinge their jaws.
The two halves of the lower jaw aren't fused at the chin.
A stretchy ligament joins them,
so they spread wide apart.
Then each side moves on its own.
Left grips, right slides forward,
then left again.
The snake basically walks its head over its meal.
But with a mouth that full, how does it breathe?
Its windpipe opening sits low in the mouth,
and it can push forward and off to the side,
like a built-in snorkel.
And its ribs only attach to the spine,
so the whole body stretches around the meal.
