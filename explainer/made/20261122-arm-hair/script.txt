Why does your arm hair stop growing,
but your head hair keeps going for years?
Every hair grows from a tiny pocket in your skin
called a follicle.
And every follicle runs on a loop.
Grow. Stop. Rest. Fall out.
Then a new hair starts in the same spot.
The trick is how long the growing part lasts.
On your head, it can run two to seven years.
At about a centimeter a month,
that's long enough to reach your waist.
On your arms, it lasts just a few months.
So the hair hits its limit,
and falls out before it ever gets long.
Arm hair doesn't grow slower.
It just quits way sooner.
Your eyebrows have a short timer too.
So if your arm hair had a seven year timer,
how long would you let it grow?
