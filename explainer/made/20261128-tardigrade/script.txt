You'd pass out in 15 seconds in space.
How did this animal last 10 days?
Meet the tardigrade, or water bear,
half a millimeter long, with eight chubby legs.
When its home dries out,
it pulls in its legs,
squeezes out almost all its water,
and curls into a tiny ball called a tun.
Inside, special proteins set like glass,
holding every cell in place,
and its body switches off.
In 2007, scientists flew dried tardigrades into orbit.
Back on Earth, they added water,
and most woke up within half an hour.
They can take a thousand times more radiation than you.
So how long would you last up there?
