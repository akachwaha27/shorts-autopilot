Pandas waste eighty-three percent of the bamboo they eat.
Here's why you never see it.
Inside, a panda has a meat-eater's gut,
built just like a bear's:
short, simple intestines,
and no cow-style stomach chambers.
So bamboo rushes straight through,
and most of it comes out in less than a day.
So pandas eat for up to fourteen hours a day,
crunching up to thirty-eight kilos of bamboo.
And they save energy like a phone on low battery.
Their thyroid runs slow,
their brain and liver are small,
and they nap a lot.
A panda burns barely forty percent of the energy
you'd expect for its size.
Scientists think it all started
when their ancestors lost a gene
for tasting meat's savory flavor.
Could you eat just one food every day, like a panda?
