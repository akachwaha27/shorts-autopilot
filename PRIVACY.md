# Privacy Policy: Content Autopilot

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Content Autopilot is a personal automation tool run by its owner. It uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's own channels: the **ClipReels_Org** YouTube channel, plus its linked Instagram and Facebook accounts and the owner's other brand accounts. It is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the owner signs in to it.

## What data the app accesses
- **YouTube:** permission to upload videos (`youtube.upload`) and read basic channel information (`youtube.readonly`), only on channels the owner signs in with.
- **Meta (Instagram/Facebook):** permission to publish posts to Pages and Instagram accounts the owner manages.

## How the data is used and stored
- The sign-in tokens are stored only as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets in the owner's private repository. They are used only to upload scheduled videos and their titles and captions.
- The app does not collect, sell or share personal data about viewers or any other person.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking is added by the app.

## Google API Services
The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube access is subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Removing access
The owner can revoke access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and in Facebook under Settings → Business Integrations. Deleting the repository's secrets removes all stored tokens.
