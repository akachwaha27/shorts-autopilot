Shorts Autopilot Privacy Policy

Shorts Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information the Shorts Autopilot application ("the app") accesses, how it is used, stored and protected, and how you can revoke access. The app is a personal tool operated by its owner (contact: ak.business427@gmail.com) to publish videos to the owner's own channels.

1. Information the app accesses

  • Google user data: with your consent, the app requests the single OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. This lets it upload videos to the YouTube channel of the account that signed in. The app does not request or read your name, email address, contacts, files, watch history, analytics, comments or any other Google data.
  • OAuth tokens: Google issues an access token and a refresh token after you consent.
  • Public data: the app reads publicly available trend information (for example, YouTube's public "most popular" video list) to choose video topics. This is not personal data.

2. How the information is used

The youtube.upload permission is used only to upload videos that the owner has generated and approved, together with their title, description and tags. Tokens are used only to authenticate those uploads. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, sale, or training AI models.

3. Storage and security

The refresh token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub Actions configuration and is accessible only to the owner's automation. The app has no database of users and stores no Google user data beyond this token. Generated video files are stored in the owner's repository.

4. Sharing

Google user data and tokens are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platforms the owner chooses to publish on (YouTube, and optionally Instagram and TikTok via their official APIs).

5. Retention and deletion

The token is kept only while the automation is active. To delete it, the owner removes the secret from GitHub, and anyone can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the token. Requests for deletion can be sent to ak.business427@gmail.com and are handled within 30 days.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Shorts Autopilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: ak.business427@gmail.com
