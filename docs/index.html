Shorts Autopilot

Shorts Autopilot is a personal video-publishing tool. It turns trending, family-friendly topics into original 30–60 second educational videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What Shorts Autopilot does

  • Finds popular, non-controversial topics each day from public trend data.
  • Writes an original script, adds an AI voiceover, captions and licensed stock footage.
  • Sends the finished video to the owner for review.
  • After approval, uploads the video to the owner's YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Why it requests Google access

Shorts Autopilot asks for one Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, used only to upload approved videos to the channel of the account that signed in. It does not read your email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Who can use it

Shorts Autopilot is used only by its owner for their own channel. No account or login is needed to read this page.
